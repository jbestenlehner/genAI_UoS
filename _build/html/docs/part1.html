Generative AI as an assistive technology#

Objective: Use Google Gemini as an assistive tool to rephrase emails, time management and support learning.

1. Getting Started#

After each exercise open a new chat in Google-Gemini (left hand side). Text formatted like this:

are examples to copy and paste into the Gemini chat window.

Gemini has 3 options:

  1. Flash: All-around help

  2. Thinking: Solves complex problems

  3. Pro: Advanced reasoning (good for coding)

Thinking and Pro have less tokens available per day.

2. Tone of email#

Ask Gemini to rewrite this email in a more approachable tone, e.g.

explain how I can rewrite this email in a more appropriate and
professional tone?

Hi <Staff member name>,

Where are the recordings for the March lecture of CMV123? We all need them to study, and it's been 12 days since the lecture.

I can not do the reading without the lecture.

Thanks
<Student name>

Thoughts:

  1. Is it too formal or informal?

  2. What would you change?

  3. Try different tones, e.g. friendly or casual tone, professional and enthusiastic, clear and neutral, appreciative tone, sincere tone, encouraging tone, …

3. Planning a revision schedule#

Can you plan me a revision schedule for Monday to Friday including
breaks? I need to revise for BioMedical Engineering exams. I am in
university 10am till 2pm on Monday and Thursday and all day on
Wednesday. I work on Monday and Thursday from 4pm till 10pm. I have
sports activities on Tuesday and Friday from 6pm to 8pm.

Personalisation:

  1. Revise the schedule to your personal need.

  2. Would the schedule work for you? If no, why not.

  3. Are the suggested topics for revision in agreement with the lecture?

  4. What would you need to change? Copy and edit the schedule manually to your personal needs.

4. Creating revision tasks#

I have an exam on nanoscience, what tasks can I use to revise?

Points to consider:

  1. Does the suggestions agree with the content of your lecture?

  2. Which points would you consider in your revision?

  3. Search for references, e.g.

    Please provide academic references for where you have taken this
information:

    • Are those references sensible?

    • Are you able to verify them?

More on verifying sources “Responsible & Ethical Use of Generative AI”.

5. Literature review for Essay#

You would like to write an essay on the properties of graphene, and you ask Gemini, e.g.

What are the properties of graphene?

Refining your literature search:#

  1. Identify references, e.g. Please provide academic references.

  2. How recent are those references?

  3. Gemini often provides fairly dated and not up-to-date references, because the training data are often a few years old. Ask Gemini for more recent references or ask to give suggestions how to find them.

Verifying references:#

  1. Check if the reference exists.

  2. What is “arXiv”? Always check the comment, if the manuscript has been accepted by a reputable peer-review journal before consideration. Preferable cite the journal reference, if it has been already published.

  3. Even though Wikipedia cannot be used as a reference, articles often include and list potential interesting/relevant references at the bottom of the article.

Database search:#

For a comprehensive literature review databases like Scopus, Dimensions, … are essential. However, a general search for “graphene” will provide many results. To focus your search, ask Gemini for keywords and/or alternative keywords on the topic of graphene. Choose keywords according to your interests and combine them with Boolean operators “AND” and “OR” in your, e.g. Scopus or Web of Science database search.

6. Summarising long text#

In your previous task you should have found an interesting publication. Try to summarise the introduction and extract key points, e.g.:

Can you summarise the following text into 5 themes?

<copy and paste here the introduction of your publication>

More on this in “Prompt Engineering & Token Efficiency”.

7. Feedback on Written Work#

  1. Ask Gemini to provide feedback on your written work.

  2. Ask Gemini to check your grammar and spelling.

  3. Gemini will provide suggestions and often rewrite your text. Be careful that you do not lose your personal character as it can be considered as a case of Academic Misconduct.
