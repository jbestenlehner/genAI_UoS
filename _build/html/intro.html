Types of AI:#

  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning (neural networks): learning from data, data analysis and modelling.

  • Natural Language Processing: chatbots, language translation, …

  • Computer Vision: self-driving cars, facial recognition, medical image analysis, …

  • Robotics: design, operation and application of robots.

  • Generative AI: Large Language Models (deep learning models), create new content.

What is Generative AI?#

  • Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI) refers to a type of artificial intelligence that focuses on creating new content.

  • It is trained on data and generates new data that has similar characteristics.

  • New content can take various forms, including:

    • Text: Generating articles, stories, poems, or code.

    • Images: Creating new pictures, artwork, or realistic photos.

    • Audio: Producing music, sound effects, or speech.

    • Video: Generating new video clips or animations.

    • 3D models: creating 3 dimensional digital objects.

Acknowledge/Describe/Evidence#

  • Acknowledge: Select a statement as appropriate to acknowledge how you used the identified GenAI tool in your work.

  • Describe: Provide a short summary to describe how you employed the tool and how you adapted or developed its outputs.

  • Evidence: Provide evidence of how you have used GenAI by providing your prompts and copying and pasting outputs

AI, Assistive Technology and unfair means#

  • Using Generative AI isn’t unfair means, it depends how it has been used in the context of the assessment.

  • Where you have used a tool or service to generate work that is not your own, but intentionally pass it off as yours, that will be considered as using unfair means.

  • However, this gives room for interpretation.

  • This module uses a traffic light.

  • It is okay to tick all the boxes, if you used it.

University of Sheffield approved tools#

Google-Gemini#

Warning

Identifying resources/literature: Carefully check, that they are correct, exist, up to data, …

  • The University of Sheffield has a privacy agreement with Google, that any data or chat conversation with Gemini are not used to train their models.

Warning

Be careful what you share/submit: No personal details, sensitive information/data, unpublished research, …

  • Anything you share could be used to train the GenAI model.

NotebookLM#

  • Upload trusted documents and/or learning materials, and NotebookLM will answer detailed questions or surface key insights.

  • Convert complex material into easy-to-understand formats like FAQs or briefing Docs.

  • Add key resources to a notebook for sharing with others to create a group knowledge base.

  • Create mind maps, quizzes, flash cards, …
