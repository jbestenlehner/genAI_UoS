# NotebookLM to support learning

**Objective:** Using NotebookLM to help with understanding complex topics and exam preparation. 

In <a href="https://notebooklm.google/" target="_blank">NotebookLM</a> you are able to create your own collection of resources. This can be very helpful when writing a Literature Review.

## 1. Becoming familiar with an unfamiliar, complex topic

Open <a href="https://notebooklm.google/" target="_blank">NotebookLM</a> and add the following publication on <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666542523000024?via" target="_blank">Graphyne</a> as PDF to your notebook. Keep the website and PDF open in your browser for overview, easier browsing and straight forward access of references or citations:

1. Start to interact with the document through the chat or by creating a mind map.
2. What are the advantages of graphyne compared to graphene? What are the structural differences?
3. Figure 2 contains a lot of information. Ask in the chat 
    ```
    What is shown on Figure 2? Please write alt-text for the figure.
    ```
4. On the middle panel you see the responses and information with reference to where they are taken from your documents. If you click on it, the highlighted text will appear on the left panel indicating where the information is taken from. The highlighted text section might contain references, which you could add to your notebook for a more comprehensive reply to specific questions on this topic.
5. Scientific publications often expect a lot of fundamental prior knowledge. For example, if you ask 
    ```
    What is a bandgap?
    ```
    the answer will be unsatisfactory. Add additional resources to your notebook, e.g. physic/chemical/biological/engineering books, lecture notes or even wikipedia articles. You can always activate or deactivate resources on the left panel.
6. Explore NotebookLM and its capabilities, e.g. tutorials, NotebookLM Help, podcast, flash cards, quizzes, ...

## 2. Revising for Exams

NotebookLM can be also used to prepare for exams. Add all resources from a lecture to a notebook and interact with the materials:

1. Create and explore the mind-map of the lecture, course or module.
2. Are you able to structure, identify and access the information, you want or need?
3. Ask NotebookLM to create quiz or exam questions.
4. Explore options to access, summarise and present content and information.
5. Is NotebookLM a useful tool for you to support your learning and exam preparation?
